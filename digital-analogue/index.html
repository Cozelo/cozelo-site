Why AI needs this before anything else

What an AI knows decides what it can do.

Context engineering is the practice of deciding what a model sees while it works: which information, in what structure, in what order and at what moment. Give a model too little and it falls back on the average of everything it has read, which is where generic work comes from. Give it too much, or several versions that disagree, and it spends tokens sorting through the pile and drifts from what your best people would say.

Good context is the smallest set of high-signal information a task needs, structured so the model can find it quickly and trust what it finds. Everything on this page is built on that idea.

Everything AI needs to know about your go-to-market, in one place.

The Digital Analogue Context Base follows the operating model Cozelo uses with every client: Message, Attract, Listen, Engage and Learn, with the facts, people, processes, technology and data underneath them.
