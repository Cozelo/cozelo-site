What an AI knows decides what it can do.

Context engineering is the practice of deciding what a model sees while it works. Give a model too little and it falls back on the average of everything it has read, which is where generic work comes from. Good context is the smallest set of high-signal information a task needs, structured so the model can find it quickly and trust what it finds.

Point a model at an uncurated shared drive and it finds the outdated deck, the abandoned pricing sheet and three competing versions of your positioning, then treats each one as fact. Every request spends tokens sorting through the noise, and the answers drift from what your best people would say.

Your data stays where it already lives.

Files stay in your storage
Your context base is a set of markdown files in your organization’s Google Drive or SharePoint. Cozelo keeps no copy of its content. Our servers hold only what the app needs to run: your organization’s name, members’ email addresses and roles, and folder and file IDs.
Narrow access
On Google Drive, the app can open only files it creates and files your team chooses in Google’s own file picker. On SharePoint, it can open only the folder your IT admin grants it, and it can’t change who has access. Every action uses the signed-in person’s own access.
Your sign-in, your platform
People sign in with their company Google or Microsoft account. Sign-in tokens stay in an encrypted cookie in the person’s browser and expire within 7 days. Anyone can sign out of every device at once.
No AI calls from the app
The app never sends your content to an AI model. Your own AI tool reads the files through its Google Drive or Microsoft 365 connector, under your AI account’s terms.
