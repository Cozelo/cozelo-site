A context tool without curated context is an engine without fuel.

Context has become one of the most talked-about words in AI, and context platforms, connectors and retrieval tools are multiplying. Each one can only work with what it’s given. Point a model at an uncurated shared drive and it finds the outdated deck, the abandoned pricing sheet and three competing versions of your positioning, then treats each one as fact. Every request spends tokens sorting through the noise, and the answers drift from what your best people would say.

Phase one builds the fuel: a curated Digital Analogue™ of your go-to-market, holding the answers your team has agreed on, organized for the AI tools you already use.
