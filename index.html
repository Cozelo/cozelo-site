Let's talk

Build the context that makes AI work like your experts.

The AI tool your team uses works from what it’s given, so results vary with each person’s expertise and aren’t shared.

Cozelo builds your Digital Analogue™: one curated, current, digital version of your business, from why buyers choose you to how your team sells. It becomes the primary source of context for every AI tool your go-to-market team uses.

AI can deliver the impact you need. But first it needs your context.

Leaders feel the gap in different ways. AI spend keeps climbing with no result to show for it. Each person’s best results stay with that person. Output looks better but takes just as long to get right, and it rarely sounds like your firm. Deals are harder to win, and the answers that used to land no longer do.

These share one cause. Your positioning lives in a presentation. Your customer knowledge is in your best minds. Your processes span systems and teams, guided by unwritten rules. Each AI tool sees only the part of that its user thinks to give it, and a shared drive hands it every outdated deck and competing version as fact.

Curated context closes the gap. When every AI tool starts from the same picture of your business, good results stop depending on who wrote the prompt.

What Cozelo builds.

Cozelo gives every AI tool your teams use a single version of the truth about your business: your Digital Analogue™.

At its center is why you win, the reason buyers choose you, and if your teams don’t share one clear answer, we research and define it first. Where the processes, systems or data around it are weak, we fix those too.
